What you need on site

A starting setup for a school or college campus.

Edge processorNVIDIA Jetson Orin, or a server with an RTX 4060/4070, for 4–8 live camera feeds.
BLE beaconsMinew or Estimote, placed every 15–20 m along corridors.
CamerasAt least 2 MP at 30 fps, mounted at eye level at entry points, not on the ceiling.

Face data and student location are sensitive. Before any real deployment, get consent from students and parents, limit who can see location, and follow your local data-protection rules.
